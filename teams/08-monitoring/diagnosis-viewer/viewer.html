NFC / NFKC comparison — A5 and G4 guidance

A5: an NFC difference is a candidate for review. Canonically equivalent forms should normally look and mean the same thing.
Under DISEASES.md, record any observed change in appearance or meaning, with original offsets and both sequences.
For example: U+0C95 U+0CC6 U+0CC2 → U+0C95 U+0CCA (ಕೊ).
